How IP targeting works

We pick the addresses. You show up on their screens.

It works like direct mail, except the mailbox is every screen on the Wi-Fi: the smart TV, the laptops, the tablets and the phones.

01

Build the list.

Your customers or CRM, a list of target accounts, or the homes and businesses that fit your buyer: homeowners in chosen ZIP codes, companies by industry and size.

02

Match to households.

Every home and office router has an IP address, and data partners link mailing addresses to the connections that serve them. Some won't match (new construction, shared networks), so your matched audience is always a little smaller than your list. We tell you the number before launch.

03

Reach every screen.

When an ad slot opens on a device behind one of those connections, the platform recognizes it and bids. Your spot plays on their smart TV, then follows on their laptop, tablet and phone, across thousands of apps and websites.

Privacy and honest limitations

The fine print,in big print.

Being straight about how this works builds more trust than overselling it. Here's what to know before you spend a dollar.

Privacy

Households, not people.

IP targeting reaches a home or business internet connection, not a named person. No names or personal details are shown to ad buyers or attached to the ads.

Cookieless isn't lawless.

Some state privacy laws, including California's and Virginia's, treat IP addresses as personal information. We work only with platforms that honor opt-outs and comply with current state laws.

Your list stays yours.

Customer lists are handled under a data agreement, hashed or processed securely, and never resold.

Plan around these

  1. Not every address matches.Match rates vary by list quality and platform, so your matched audience will be smaller than your list. Clean lists match best.
  2. IP addresses change.Internet providers reassign them, so platforms refresh their address-to-IP links regularly.
  3. Shared networks blur the target.Apartments, campuses, hotels and some mobile carriers put many people behind one IP address.
  4. Phones leave the house.Phones are reachable at home on Wi-Fi. Away from home, reach depends on the platform's device linking.
  5. Small lists limit delivery.A few hundred addresses may not spend a meaningful budget, so we pair small B2B lists with venue retargeting or lookalikes.
  6. It needs time.Awareness takes repetition. Plan on at least three months before judging results.
